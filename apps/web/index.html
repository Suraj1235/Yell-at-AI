Same words. Different meaning.
Your voice already knew.

No model weights, no API key, no server for the analysis. A lightweight signal-processing pass pulls grounded cues straight from the waveform, in this page, and labels them with a confidence — so the assistant can see the evidence, not guess at your mood. (Turning speech into words is the exception — the badge on the recorder names the engine and who receives your audio.)

  • Emphasis

    Which word you leaned on, scored against the rest of the sentence as a stress z-score.

  • Urgency & pace

    Fast, high-energy delivery with few pauses — a signal to act before over-explaining.

  • Hesitation

    Filled pauses and gaps that say you're thinking out loud, not handing down a spec.

  • Uncertainty

    A rising tail on a statement — the "...right?" your transcript silently dropped.

  • Intensity

    Very high energy and a tense voice quality. When you're actually yelling, it says so.

  • Affect

    One grounded emotional reading with an interpretation the assistant can respond to.

      The part that makes this product work — the signal processing, the emphasis scoring, the
      affect reading, the whole vocalcontext/v1 contract — is computed in your browser
      from your recording. There is no server behind it and no model weights to load. The same
      engine ships in the command-line tool, the editor adapters, and the desktop app.
    
      
    
      
      Transcription is the honest exception. To turn your speech into words, this
      page uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API — and in Chrome and Edge, that uploads your
      audio to Google's servers for recognition. We will not pretend otherwise. The badge above the
      recorder always names the engine in use, and names the company that receives your audio when
      one does — before you press record, not after.
